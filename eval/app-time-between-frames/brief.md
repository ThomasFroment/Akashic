Problem: turn Akashic's sparse stored observations (frames stored only when the picture changed, per-monitor "shown" runs whose checked_ms advances on every unchanged check, stored no-recording gaps) into continuous per-app focused time, so the timeline shows which app was in front for nearly all recorded time and per-app durations match reality (audit #196).
Goals: (1) the timeline shows which app was in front for nearly all recorded time, with no untyped holes (M5.1 holes today: 116/110 s per 300 s smoke, 582 s per standard hour, 5,240 s over the 7 h day run); (2) per-app durations match truth (M5.3 duration error today 0.18-0.59); (3) no app time is counted across lock, sleep, pause, stop or away-from-keyboard.
Fidelity: must: M5.1 holes at most 5 % of truth app time on the standard and day runs; must: M5.2 overlap no higher than today; must: never extend an app band into a stored no-recording gap or past the end of recording; must: M5.3 duration error below today on every run. may fake: a band's end up to one check interval late (5 s focused, 15 s others); may fake: idle/away detection approximated where no input signal is stored; may fake: game sessions keep today's handling.
Budget: derivation at query/episode time on stored rows, at most 50 ms per day of data on one core of a Ryzen 7 7800X3D; capture may add a cheap new record (e.g. input-idle from GetLastInputInfo, or foreground-change rows) costing at most 1 ms per capture tick and a few KB per hour; no new dependency (numpy, stdlib, ctypes/comtypes/winrt already present); schema changes allowed (fresh app, no migration).
Non-goals: game detection itself (#188), media sessions and episodes, the timeline UI, multi-monitor visible-but-not-focused time (TODO #149), retention, Ask/answer quality.
Explored: hold each session to the next session's start with a fixed cap of 60-300 s (holes 0.55/0.0 s, error 0.318/0.321 on smoke, but leaves a hole after any static stretch longer than the cap; refuted for static pages); hold bounded by the last-frame monitor's "unchanged" confirmation (monitor_states.checked_ms + 5 s), the next start, stored gaps, game end and end of recording (holes 1.6/0.3 s, error 0.231/0.320 on smoke; unmeasured on idle, lock, sleep or long static screens); see docs/research/app-usage-time-from-samples.md and eval/quality/spikes/session_hold_replay.py.
