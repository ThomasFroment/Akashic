# Quality baseline 2026-11-18

What Akashic records and derives, measured by the `eval/quality/` harness on the target PC, at the
2026-11-18 audit's base code (`src/` as of 7d6d213; the harness changes since are in `eval/` only).
Metric definitions: [audit scope](../audits/2026-11-18-quality/scope.md). Raw results, truth logs
and per-cell coverage: `eval/quality/results/<run-id>/`. Numbers are comparable only with runs of
the same machine id.

Machine `target`: Ryzen 7 7800X3D, 32 GB, RTX 4080 SUPER 16 GB; primary 3840x2160 at 125 %,
right and left 2560x1440; Wave:3 mic, DT-770 PRO default output, Wave Link virtual endpoints,
VB-CABLE as the harness's mic; Windows OCR en-US and fr-FR.

Repeatability: `run.py compare` lists 68 values more than 5 % apart, nearly all timings (job
delays, frame-time max and p99, capture delays) and splits with n ≤ 8; the headline rates above
agree within 5 % except M3.3 (0.59 vs 0.68), M1.2 useless ratio and M9.3 (n = 3). Smoke is too
short for the 5 % rule on small-n values; the acceptance check is the standard pair.

What these smoke numbers already say (to explain in Phase 2, not yet findings):

- **Akashic lists itself as a sound source** (M7.1, 17 of 17 system segments): its loopback
  capture is an audible session on the recorded endpoint, and `_audible`
  (`src/akashic/platform/win/audio.py:544`) has no own-process filter.
- **App time is cut to stored frames** (M5.1 about 110 of 300 s holes, M5.3 error 0.57): an app
  session ends at its last stored frame (`pipeline/sessions.py:53-73`), so with the 60 s game-mode
  screenshot interval the game shows as instants.
- **Games split on every voice-chat visit and then vanish** (M6.1, M11.1 game F1 0): the 0.9-105.9 s
  game was stored as two rows around a Discord visit, neither reaching the 2 min episode minimum.
- **Overlapping speech is mostly lost** (M9.1 overlap WER 0.75, M8.1 overlap recall 0.41): a
  French video under an English call keeps one language.
- **Small fonts and 1440p side monitors** read badly (M3.1 CER 0.41-0.45 at ≤ 11 device px, 0.40 at
  1440p vs 0.02-0.04 at 4K); deferred game-mode OCR CER 0.47-0.51.
- **Media rows open at the first poll after play** (M6.1 0.09-0.26 s). Revised 2026-11-19: the first scoring read 16 s because the video started during the scenario's 60 s Pause, when Akashic records nothing by design; the scorer now moves a truth start inside a no-recording gap to its end.
- **Guardrails while gaming**: worker and llama-server hold about 4.3 + 4.7 GB of VRAM;
  synthetic game p99 frame time 0.61 → 1.02-1.08 ms with Akashic (uncapped at about 3,800 fps).
- Privacy held: 0 canary hits in SQL, frames, logs, temp files and the data folder, 0 plaintext
  files, 0 outbound connections.

New in standard and day (to take into Phases 4-5, not yet audit findings):

- **Transcription stops for good after many model swaps** (day): the worker swaps Whisper and
  llama-server whenever the job kind changes (`JobQueue.claim` is oldest-first across kinds,
  `JobRunner._run` evicts the other model each time): 134 Whisper loads in about 2 h, then a load
  raised `ValueError` at 08:16 and every retry failed; 1,737 transcribe jobs waited until the worker
  was killed by hand at 13:49 (the supervisor restarted it and the backlog drained in about 45 min).
  M12.1 transcribe delay p50 9,950 s is that stall. VRAM in use fell from 9.9 to 4.7 GB when the old
  worker died.
- **Capture VRAM grows all day**: the capture tree's VRAM rose steadily from 0.70 GB (06:09) to
  4.52 GB (12:45), about 0.6 GB/h (G2 capture p95 0.54 GB smoke, 1.02 GB standard, 4.43 GB day).
- **A privacy canary reached stored frames**: M10.1 1 hit in standard (4 frames) and day
  (74 frames), inside the canary window's rectangle; 0 in smoke. Which canary and why is not
  analysed yet.
- **Games fragment into many stored rows**: M6.1 9 stored game rows per truth session in standard,
  50 in day (Outer Wilds and the synthetic game reopen a row on each return).
- **Video episodes** F1 drops from 1 (standard) to 0.12 (day); title accuracy 0.53.
- **App-time holes** (M5.1) 582 s in standard and 5,240 s in day, as smoke predicted (#196).
- Store growth about 4.2 GB per day of use (G4).
- Scoring is slow: 55 min for standard and about 3 h for day, single core.
