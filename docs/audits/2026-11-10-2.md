# Audit 2026-11-10-2: solution quality

See the [measured baseline](../baselines/2026-11-11.md).
The evaluation/test fixes completed since this review are recorded there. Findings and source
references below remain the historical review; superseded documents are not kept in this repository.

Base: the pre-audit code (the first audit's report added no code).
Second audit of the day. It covers how well the solution works: accuracy, reliability, inference and
evidence quality. It does not cover speed or system resources, which `docs/audits/2026-11-10.md` and the
`docs/solutions/` reports cover. Finding numbers continue from the first audit (#113 onward).
Findings marked (recurring) extend an open item of that audit.

Method:
- **Review:** one reviewer per quality dimension: evaluation methodology, speech, capture, reliability,
  memory (episodes), and answer accuracy. Each judged its area against the two target questions.
- **Verification:**
  - Four adversarial refuters checked every MUST. They read the code and ran small stdlib experiments
    in scratch directories: the `Segmenter`, the time resolver and planner rules, SQLite rowid reuse,
    and the eval substring scorers.
  - They also checked the faster-whisper skip rule against its source.
  - SHOULD findings were not refuted; none of them removes code.
- **Merging:** duplicates across reviewers were merged at the most severe location.

Top 5 by impact on the target questions:

1. **#175, #177: the two target questions fail inside a chat thread, or return arbitrary results.**
   - Ask "What did Minlo say … about a game?" and then "What video did I watch yesterday afternoon?" in
     the same chat. The second question inherits Minlo + "game" and finds nothing (#175).
   - Asked on its own, the video question ranks videos by similarity to an empty topic, keeps 5, and
     presents them as the complete list (#177).
2. **#164: after deletions, no new games, videos or conversations are built.** Source ids are reused
   once the newest rows are deleted. Old episode links and refs then block every new build, and the
   conversation builder stalls its watermark for good. There is no error.
3. **#154, #155, #157: processing can stop silently while the tray says "Recording".**
   - A job that crashes the worker is re-run first forever and blocks the queue (#154).
   - The "Run waiting jobs now" retry marks failed transcriptions done without transcribing (#155).
   - The worker state is never surfaced (#157).
4. **#165-#167: video episodes are wrong in three ways.**
   - They store "Google Chrome" instead of `chrome.exe`, so app filters never match (#165).
   - Edge videos never get their soundtrack transcript (#166).
   - Up to 10 minutes of pause are counted as watching time, pulling unrelated audio in (#167).
5. **#113-#118: the evaluations do not measure what they are cited for.**
   - The 29-question eval exercises a legacy search path that production no longer uses (#113).
   - Fact, abstention and citation scoring accept wrong answers (#114-#116).
   - The STT reference is pre-filled with the incumbent's output (#117).
   - 29/29 and 20/20 are development-set scores (#118).
