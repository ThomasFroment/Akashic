# Audit 2026-11-10

See the [measured baseline](../baselines/2026-11-11.md).
Findings, queues and source references below describe the audited revision; they are not a live
completion list. Superseded documents are not kept in this repository.

Base: the pre-audit code. First audit, so no recurring findings.

Method: one reviewer per dimension, then adversarial refuters on every MUST, every Approaches item, every
removal and every recommended alternative. Nothing was executed: no `.venv` exists and `uv` is not
installed on this machine, so every finding was argued from code, SQLite source and primary docs.

Top 5 by impact on the target questions and on the privacy rules:

1. **#30 + #31** Without the speaker models, every speakers job stays pending forever. That freezes the
   raw-audio retention, and the shared transcript horizon then stops **every** episode builder, so no new
   video, game or app episode is ever built. "What video did I watch yesterday?" stops working silently.
2. **#16, #17, #18** Capture exclusions fail open: the password-field check is skipped on a second monitor
   and on UIA errors, and media titles from excluded (finance or user-listed) sites still reach the media log.
   AGENTS.md rule 2 is not met in these paths.
3. **#45** "il y a 2h" / "2h ago" resolves to 02:00 today, so the most natural French time anchor
   searches the wrong window.
4. **#46** Merging a voice into a named speaker (for example into "Minlo") removes that person from every
   episode the voice was in. Line evidence survives, but episode-level person search does not.
5. **#60** Lock or Quit racing a pending Settings restart restarts capture and the worker while the app sits
   at the unlock prompt.

Best alternatives found (verified, see Alternatives): a Qwen3-Reranker through the existing llama-server
(#105), word-level timestamps for speaker attribution (#106), and plain `onnxruntime` without the CUDA/cuDNN 13
wheels (#107, about 1.2 GB of compressed wheels removed and the `override-dependencies` hack gone).
Numbering skips #59.
